# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Researchers and academic writers who evaluate, revise, or draft scientific paragraphs while working inside a ResearchHub project.

## Product Purpose

Writing Lab supports evidence-aware academic writing in focused, reviewable steps. Success means writers can trace suggestions to the supplied text, preserve the original meaning, and make the final decisions themselves.

## Brand Personality

Professional, calm, and precise. The interface should feel like a connected HyperDataLab research tool and keep attention on the writer's source text and next action.

## Anti-references

- Avoid oversized marketing-style hero sections inside the writing workspace.
- Avoid decorative effects, saturated inactive states, or presenting model suggestions as verified facts.
- Avoid visual language that conflicts with ResearchHub and Paragraph Lab.

## Design Principles

1. Put the current writing task and its next action first.
2. Keep evaluations anchored to exact source text.
3. Make AI suggestions, evidence, and writer decisions visually distinct.
4. Preserve original text and make revisions reviewable.
5. Reuse familiar navigation, controls, and status colors across HyperDataLab tools.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, keyboard navigation, semantic controls, and visible focus. Never rely on color alone for status; respect reduced motion and preserve English source text exactly.
